How I Think

The reasoning behind the results.

Career tells what happened; here I tell the reasoning behind it.

How I prioritize

Turnaround at Rappi · downsizing at Porto

Priority is reach, importance and effort, weighed at the right level.

  • Reach (scope it touches), importance (result it moves), effort (resources it takes).
  • More weight when the impact is at the company level, not just my area.
  • Top: high reach with proportional effort. The urgent never hijacks the essential.

How I structure problems

OKRs + FCA management · iFood and Decolar

Fact, Cause and Action: it moves decisions from opinion to what can be measured.

  • Fact: an unquestionable, measured data point, never a perception.
  • Cause: the real root I'm responsible to act on, never an external one.
  • Action: measurable and tied directly to the Fact's indicator.

How I turn data into decisions

ABCD matrix at Serasa · dashboards at VOLL

From reliable data to the message that decides.

  • A single source of truth (OSST); if it doesn't exist, I build it.
  • I separate signal from noise and answer one objective business question.
  • I close with a visualization carrying ONE clear message, in the decider's language.

How I use AI

AI adoption at Decolar (Toqan, Claude Code)

AI as a multiplier, with judgment always mine.

  • Automate: repetitive, low-risk tasks that are easy to validate.
  • Analyze: recurring routines over reliable pipelines, pre-agreed with the users.
  • Complex: skills that adapt AI to each business context.

How I present results

Localiza and Despegar boards · Rappi HQ

Before building, three questions.

  • Purpose: explain the past, project scenarios, or unlock a decision?
  • The audience's framework: financial, problem-solving, or collaborative?
  • Stakeholders: what I expect from each, with deadline and tone. Lead to the decision, don't just show data.

How I balance speed and governance

Innovation with control · iFood and Decolar

Holes in the ship, yes; below the waterline, never.

  • I define where control is non-negotiable and where failing fast is welcome.
  • I keep the team in the OK zone: bureaucracy doesn't kill innovation, lack of process doesn't cause misalignment.
  • Goal: self-alignment with high autonomy.
