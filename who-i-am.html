Who I Am

Heitor Mobílio

I've spent the past decade on the frontier between strategy and execution: the work of making complex decisions clear, funded, and, above all, executed. An economist from UNICAMP, I began in Finance at Bosch and Serasa Experian and built a track record leading FP&A and Strategy at digital-first companies and marketplaces. Today I'm part of Landa Club Cohort 20, a community of entrepreneurs and consultants focused on technology and new business in Latin America, and a certified board director through Board Academy, ready to contribute on boards, including more junior seats.

How I create value

I connect strategy and execution: turning analysis into decision, and decision into coordinated movement across teams. My edge is no longer in producing the number. It's in asking the right question, providing the business context, and uniting teams around the decision. With AI taking on much of the analysis, my value concentrates in that judgment and orchestration.

How I think

I structure problems by Fact, Cause and Action, and prioritize by reach, importance, and effort. The full mental model, with the frameworks for data, AI, presentation, and governance, is in How I Think.

What I look for

Digital-first companies in growth, including those going through transformation under PE/VC, where strategy and execution must move together: growing with governance, innovating with control, scaling without losing predictability. It's the context where a Planning and Strategy function stops being support and becomes the axis that sustains growth.
