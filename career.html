Decolar

Sr. Planning & Strategy Manager · 2026

Context

Brazil's leading OTA, post-acquisition by the Prosus group, amid a culture shift and growth acceleration.

Challenge

Establish Planning and Strategy governance and the management model for the Brazil B2C P&L to sustain growth and secure the strategic transition.

Actions

Restructured the team under the Prosus culture, consolidated the management model by reworking the strategy-execution rituals, accelerated AI adoption, and consolidated the reporting pack to the Despegar board.

Impact

New management rituals in place, with OKRs aligned to growth targets; recognized as the sponsor of the AI agenda in Brazil.

Lessons

Expanding Finance into strategy demands excellence in reporting fundamentals, even under shifting leadership and culture. First earn trust through technical depth, then expand the impact.

VOLL

Head of FP&A · 2024–2025

Context

B2B traveltech (Localiza group) in rapid expansion, with +40% in three months, demanding financial governance.

Challenge

Build reliable governance and forecasting to sustain growth without Finance being a bottleneck, amid team seniority upgrades and an ERP migration.

Actions

Built post-ERP revenue-data auditing through quality dashboards, weekly business tracking, and pipeline management; analyzed the largest costs to protect gross margin.

Impact

EBITDA forecast accuracy from 80% to 98% and revenue from 89% to 99%; led the due diligence for Warburg Pincus's purchase of the L&Co stake.

Lessons

Reliability and predictability, paired with clear, transparent governance, are the scaffolding of healthy accelerated growth.

Evidence

Recommendation from Mirna A. Carvalho (CFO). See Recommendations.

Rappi

Sr. FP&A Manager · 2023–2024

Context

Delivery marketplace with a recent Box Delivery acquisition, betting on a new logistics model to gain market share.

Challenge

Grow with austerity: capture Box Delivery synergies and reduce the Brazil operation's burn against an established leader.

Actions

Led the turnaround across every area, as the conductor of the joint effort to deliver the result; created Box Delivery↔Rappi logistics-integration forums and weekly decision rituals.

Impact

Brazil operation to breakeven in 9 months, recognized by the Brazil CEO within 3 months; Box Delivery unit delivery cost at ~70% of the average.

Lessons

Structural course-correction, paired with growth and efficiency, is as central in times of adjustment as in times of growth.

Evidence

Recommendations from Mirna A. Carvalho and Luiz Otávio Giordani Junior. See Recommendations.

Porto · Olho Mágico

Finance & Data Analytics Manager · 2022–2023

Context

A digital venture (Olho Mágico) inside Porto (PSSA3) to enter the real-estate rental and sales market.

Challenge

Build a digital product inside a market-leading incumbent, an ambidexterity demand, then navigate the hardening macro environment.

Actions

Led the Finance and Data Analytics team, built a multidisciplinary team, and integrated NIDO Tecnologia post-M&A; ran the downsizing when viability proved unsustainable.

Impact

Built the Olho Mágico brand, with a listings portal matching the market leader's size in just 8 months; successful NIDO integration despite the adverse context.

Lessons

A first major leadership role and direct responsibility for the finances of a strategic venture in a listed group brought maturity in negotiation, executive presentation, and hard decisions.

iFood

Sr. FP&A Coordinator · 2020–2022

Context

First digital-native, mobile-first company, with direct exposure to OKR management and results-driven decisions.

Challenge

First leadership role, amid the pandemic, needing to structure the financial viability of marketing and new products.

Actions

Led restaurant relief during COVID-19, managed exclusivity contracts, optimized performance marketing in the weekly agenda with the CMO, and structured iFood Clube.

Impact

R$1bn+ injected into the cash flow of 45k+ restaurants; the financial backbone of iFood Clube, today 90% of food-delivery marketing spend; pioneered LTV/CAC.

Lessons

Agile, precise financial management in the New Economy, results-driven leadership, and structuring strategic projects.

Evidence

Recommendations from Paula Cabianca and Carolina Rodrigues. See Recommendations.

Serasa Experian

Sr. FP&A Analyst · 2018–2020

Context

A consolidated market leader in financial information, but with little efficiency analysis in Sales and Marketing.

Challenge

First experience in Finance: build the FP&A toolkit and deliver results through strong relationships.

Actions
